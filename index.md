# The Best News Ever

If your life ended today, do you know for sure where you'd spend eternity?

That may sound like a heavy question—but it’s also the most important one you could ever ask. Thankfully, God hasn’t left us in the dark. He’s given us a clear and simple answer in the Bible. The solution isn’t about trying harder, being religious, or hoping you've done enough. It’s about receiving a **free gift**.

---

<a id="god"></a>
## God Is Perfect—and He Sets the Standard

The Bible says God is absolutely holy and just. That means He is completely pure and always does what is right. Because He is just, He can’t ignore sin—He must deal with it fairly.

> “For the LORD is righteous, He loves righteousness...” — *Psalm 11:7*

To live with God forever, you’d have to meet **His** standard: perfect righteousness. And that leads to a problem…

---

<a id="problem"></a>
## Our Problem: We All Fall Short

Let’s be honest—we’ve all messed up. We’ve said, thought, and done things that don’t reflect God’s character. That’s what the Bible calls **sin**. It’s not just the “big stuff.” It’s any way we fall short of God’s perfection.

> “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” — *Romans 3:23*

Because of our sin, we are separated from God. And the penalty for sin is serious—it’s death and eternal separation from Him.

> “For the wages of sin is death…” — *Romans 6:23a*

No amount of good works, personal effort, or religious rituals can erase that debt. But here's where the good news comes in.

---

<a id="solution"></a>
## God’s Solution: Jesus Christ

Even though we couldn’t reach Him, God came to us. He sent His Son, Jesus—fully God and fully man—who lived the perfect life we never could.

Then, Jesus willingly died on a cross. In that moment, He took on Himself the full weight of all our sin—past, present, and future—and paid the penalty we owed.

> “But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.” — *Romans 5:8*

Three days later, Jesus rose from the dead. His resurrection proves the payment was complete and accepted—and that He offers **real life** beyond the grave.

---

<a id="response"></a>
## Our Response: Trust in Christ Alone

So how do you receive this gift of eternal life? It’s not by earning it. Not by promising to be better. Not by doing anything to “qualify.”

The Bible says it's simply by **believing** in Jesus—fully trusting that He is who He claimed to be (God’s Son) and that His death and resurrection are enough to save you forever.

> “For God so loved the world that He gave His only Son, that whoever **believes in Him** should not perish but have eternal life.” — *John 3:16*

Faith means being convinced that Jesus has done it all. The moment you believe in Him for eternal life, you receive it—instantly, permanently, and completely.

---

<a id="promise"></a>
## God’s Promise: You Can Know You Have Eternal Life

When you believe in Jesus, God gives you **eternal life**—not temporary life or probation. Eternal life means **forever**, and it starts the moment you believe.

Your salvation is secure—not because of your performance, but because of God’s promise and Christ’s finished work.

> “I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; no one will snatch them out of My hand.” — *John 10:28*

> “Truly, truly, I say to you, whoever believes has eternal life.” — *John 6:47*

This is not about feelings or emotions. It’s about the trustworthy promise of a faithful God.

---

## So... What About You?

Have you ever come to the point where you’ve personally believed in Jesus alone for eternal life?

If not, you can do that right now. There’s no ritual, no special prayer, no conditions—just believe in Jesus and what He did for you. If you already have, then you can rest in the confidence that eternal life is yours—forever.

---

## What’s Next?

Believing in Jesus is just the beginning of a personal relationship with God. While good works and spiritual growth don’t earn or maintain your salvation, they are a natural response to God’s incredible grace.

You can grow in your relationship with Him by:
- Reading the Bible to learn more about who God is and what He’s done,
- Talking to Him through prayer,
- Connecting with a local church that clearly teaches the truth of God’s Word and the freeness of His grace.

---

**This is the best news ever.**  
Not because you’ve done something great—but because **Jesus did it all**, and you can have eternal life simply by trusting in Him.

> “These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, that you may **know** that you have eternal life…” — *1 John 5:13*
